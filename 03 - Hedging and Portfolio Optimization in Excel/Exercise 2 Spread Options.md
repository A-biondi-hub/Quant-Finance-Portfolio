# Assignment 2 - Spread Options, Hedged Portfolio and PCA

## Setting

- **Valuation date:** 15/05/2025.
- **Riskless rate:** 3.5% per year.
- **Data:** daily stock prices; at least 6 stocks per group member, up to a maximum of 12.

## Tasks

1. **Spread option pricing.** For each asset, generate 5,000 scenarios and apply the fundamental theorem of asset pricing to price six European call spreads and six European put spreads, at the money, with a maturity of 3 months, in a Black-Scholes framework.

2. **Hedged portfolio with the STARR ratio.** Short selling is allowed.
   - The bought shares are hedged by buying European puts with a maturity of 2 months (42 trading days) and moneyness $b_i = K_i / S_{0,i}$ equal to the 3rd percentile of the $i$-th gross return.
   - The short-sold shares are hedged by buying European calls with a maturity of 2 months (42 trading days) and moneyness $b_i = K_i / S_{0,i}$ equal to the 96th percentile of the $i$-th gross return.

   Determine the optimal hedged portfolio that maximises the STARR ratio with CVaR 3%, and verify its value after 1 week (5 trading days) or after 2 months.

3. **PCA and approximated portfolios.** Using the returns from Task 2 (stocks and derivatives):
   - compute the first two principal components with a PCA applied to the correlation matrix;
   - regress each portfolio of returns on the two principal components using a conditional expectation estimator, and determine the optimal hedged portfolio that maximises the Sharpe ratio of the approximated portfolios;
   - repeat the exercise with the approximated portfolio obtained as the portfolio of the approximated returns from a linear regression model.

Plot the ex-post wealth of the optimal choices from Tasks 2 and 3, assuming an initial wealth of EUR 100,000.

## Note on the conditional expectation estimator

Since the conditioning is on two random variables (the two principal components), use two partitions, one for each component, with at least 4 sets each. Take all the intersections of the two partitions (at least 16 sets) and use, as the estimator of the conditional expectation, the portfolio conditional on the sigma-algebra generated by the intersections.
